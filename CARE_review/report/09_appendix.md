# 9. Appendices

## Appendix A — Glossary

Terms are expanded on first use in the text; this is the consolidated list.

### The interventions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CMAM** | Community-based Management of Acute Malnutrition — treating already-malnourished children at home rather than in hospital |
| **SAM** | Severe Acute Malnutrition — the immediately life-threatening form of wasting; roughly 11% under-five case mortality in Africa |
| **MAM** | Moderate Acute Malnutrition — at risk, but not in immediate danger |
| **RUTF** | Ready-to-Use Therapeutic Food — fortified paste for treating SAM at home; needs no water, refrigeration or cooking. The main commodity cost |
| **RUSF** | Ready-to-Use Supplementary Food — the lighter equivalent, for MAM |
| **LNS / SQ-LNS** | Lipid-based Nutrient Supplements; SQ = small-quantity. Used for moderate malnutrition and prevention |
| **F75 / F100** | Therapeutic milks used to stabilise complicated cases as inpatients |
| **MUAC** | Mid-Upper-Arm Circumference — colour-coded tape screening; the field diagnostic for acute malnutrition |
| **OTP** | Outpatient Therapeutic Programme — the treat-at-home service |
| **ComPAS / OptiMA** | Simplified protocols. ComPAS merges SAM and MAM into one product; OptiMA tapers RUTF dosing |
| **MMS** | Multiple Micronutrient Supplementation — the antenatal multivitamin tablet |
| **IFA** | Iron and Folic Acid — the older, cheaper antenatal tablet MMS would replace. The IFA-to-MMS switch is the central policy question |
| **UNIMMAP** | The standard 15-micronutrient MMS formulation |
| **BEP** | Balanced Energy-Protein supplementation |
| **KMC** | Kangaroo Mother Care — continuous skin-to-skin holding of the newborn. Cuts neonatal mortality 32% at high certainty |
| **EBF** | Exclusive Breastfeeding — breast milk only, no water or other food |
| **EIBF** | Early Initiation of Breastfeeding — first feed within one hour of birth |
| **BFHI** | Baby-Friendly Hospital Initiative — facility certification for breastfeeding practice |
| **IYCF** | Infant and Young Child Feeding — the standard counselling curriculum |
| **SBCC** | Social and Behaviour Change Communication — counselling, education and campaigns |

### Delivery and people

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CHW** | Community Health Worker — locally recruited, lightly trained, often part-time or unpaid. The central delivery platform in all three interventions |
| **ANC** | Antenatal Care — pregnancy check-up visits; the delivery channel for MMS |
| **iCCM** | Integrated Community Case Management — the standard community package for childhood illness |
| **LMIC** | Low- and Middle-Income Country |
| **HEW / WDA** | Health Extension Worker / Women's Development Army — Ethiopia's salaried and volunteer community cadres |
| **ASHA / AWW / ANM** | India's Accredited Social Health Activist, Anganwadi Worker and Auxiliary Nurse Midwife |
| **ICDS / POSHAN / AMB** | India's Integrated Child Development Services, POSHAN Abhiyaan and Anemia Mukt Bharat national programmes |
| **LHW** | Lady Health Worker — Pakistan's community cadre, covering about 60% of the population |
| **FCHV** | Female Community Health Volunteer — Nepal's cadre, over 50,000 volunteers |

### Measurement and evidence

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **RR** | Risk Ratio. 1.00 = no difference. Below 1 is good for bad outcomes (death, low birthweight); above 1 is good for good outcomes (recovery, breastfeeding) |
| **OR / HR** | Odds Ratio / Hazard Ratio — read like RR |
| **RD** | Risk Difference — an absolute rather than relative difference |
| **MD / SMD** | Mean Difference / Standardised Mean Difference — compares averages, where **0** rather than 1 means no difference |
| **95% CI** | Confidence interval. **If it crosses 1.00, the result is not statistically significant** |
| **N** | Number of participants |
| **RCT** | Randomised Controlled Trial |
| **IPD** | Individual Participant Data — pooling raw records rather than published summaries; stronger than a standard meta-analysis |
| **GRADE** | Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — formal certainty rating (high / moderate / low / very low) |
| **Active control** | A comparison where the control group also received treatment, so the measured benefit is against another treatment rather than against nothing |
| **PICOS** | Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcomes, Study design — the review specification template |

### Coverage, targets and outcomes

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Coverage** | Of everyone who needs an intervention, the share who receive it |
| **Adherence** | Of those who receive it, the share who use it as intended |
| **Sphere standard** | Humanitarian minimum performance benchmark; ≥75% recovery for malnutrition treatment |
| **SQUEAC / SLEAC** | The standard survey methods for measuring treatment coverage |
| **WHA targets** | World Health Assembly global nutrition goals — e.g. 70% exclusive breastfeeding |
| **Defaulting / LTFU** | Patients dropping out before completing treatment / Loss To Follow-Up |
| **Stock-out** | Supplies unavailable at the point of delivery |
| **LBW / SGA** | Low Birthweight / Small for Gestational Age |
| **SVN** | Small Vulnerable Newborn — the combined category of preterm, SGA and low-birthweight infants |
| **pp** | Percentage points |

## Appendix E — Reproducing this analysis

All numbers in this report regenerate from the evidence database.

```bash
# Retrieval and corpus assembly
python3 CARE_review/code/run_retrieval.py
python3 CARE_review/code/run_pipeline.py corpus
python3 CARE_review/code/run_pipeline.py fulltext
python3 CARE_review/code/run_pipeline.py cards
#   -> extraction workflow (one agent per batch, idempotent and resumable)
python3 CARE_review/code/run_pipeline.py merge      # -> data/evidence_db.json

# Country analysis (§4 and Appendix C)
python3 CARE_review/code/country_analysis.py        # -> data/country_analysis.json

# Verification
python3 code/17_verify_synthesis.py CARE_review/CARE_DEEPDIVE_REPORT.md \
        CARE_review/data/deepdive_corpus.json CARE_review/data/evidence_db.json

# Render to .docx
bash CARE_review/code/render_report.sh
```

Supporting documentation: `docs/PICOS_specification.md` (the review specification), `docs/PROCESS_deepdive.md` (end-to-end method), `prompts/deepdive_extraction_prompt.md` and `prompts/deepdive_synthesis_prompt.md` (the extraction schema and synthesis grounding rules, which define the method as much as the code does).

## Appendix F — Citation conventions

Sources are cited inline by persistent identifier — **PMID** for PubMed-indexed records and **DOI** otherwise — rather than by author and year. This is deliberate: the identifier is what the verifier resolves against the evidence database, so the citation in the text and the record that supports it are the same object. Every identifier in this report corresponds to a record in `data/evidence_db.json`.
